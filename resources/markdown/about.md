# About SaucePls

SaucePls is a community for finding the origins of images and sharing what you discover. Post an image as a sauce request, explore possible matches, and help others track down their sources.

## How it works

When you submit a still image, SaucePls can look for possible source matches, read visible text, and suggest descriptive tags. You can review and edit the extracted text and tags before publishing. These tools provide suggestions, not guaranteed answers, and results may be incomplete. Animated GIFs keep their animation and do not go through the full analysis process.

Beyond image discovery, SaucePls supports community tags and tag history, answers with accepted solutions, comments and likes, bookmarks, user follows and a subscription feed, profiles, notifications, search, and explicit-content controls.

## Services and credits

- [SauceNAO](https://saucenao.com/) provides candidate image-source matches.
- [Nanoskript's deepdanbooru-docker project on GitHub](https://github.com/Nanoskript/deepdanbooru-docker) powers tag and rating suggestions. SaucePls currently sends inference requests to [Nanoskript's hosted live demo API](https://deepdanbooru.nsk.sh/deepdanbooru) instead of hosting its own DeepDanbooru compute.
- [OCR.space](https://ocr.space/) provides image text recognition.
- [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/) delivers email sign-in codes, and Google provides the Google sign-in option.

These are independent third-party services. Their availability and results can vary, and their terms and usage limits apply.

## Service limits

SauceNAO is currently used on its free tier, which allows up to 100 requests per day. OCR.space is also subject to free-tier limits. Quotas, features, and availability may change according to each provider's terms, and reaching a limit can affect the related features.
